Questions

Before you buy.

Does this renew automatically, like a subscription that charges my card?

No. You pay for the period you choose, your plan runs until it ends, and you renew whenever you are ready. Nothing is ever charged automatically.

What does it cost to renew?

After your first year, renewal is charged at a very nominal price — a small fraction of the first-year price. We will remind you, with the exact amount, well before your plan ends.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — move up or down as your business changes. Contact us and we'll help you switch.

What happens when my trial or plan ends?

Your books remain available to view and export; new entries resume as soon as a plan is activated.

Is online payment available yet?

Not yet — it's coming soon. For now, contact us and we'll set your account up directly.
