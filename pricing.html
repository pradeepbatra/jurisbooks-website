Questions

Good to know before you buy.

Does this auto-renew, like a subscription that charges my card every month?

No. You pay for the period you want, your plan runs until that ends, and you renew whenever you're ready — nothing is auto-charged without you doing it.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — move up or down as your business changes. Contact us and we'll help you switch.

What happens when my trial or plan ends?

You can still see your books, but nothing new can be saved until you activate a plan.

Is online payment available yet?

Not yet — it's coming soon. For now, contact us and we'll set your account up directly.
