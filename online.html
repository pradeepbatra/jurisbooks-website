How It Works

From download to your first invoice in three steps.

01

Download and install

One installer for Windows. Nothing else to set up — no developer tools, no database, no server of your own.

02

Sign in with your mobile number

Type your number, enter the one-time code we send, and you are in. A new number starts a free 7-day trial automatically.

03

Create a company — or open one you already have

Start a new company, or sign in on another computer and open the same one: your books are already there.

What You Get

Everything in Jurisbooks, plus the parts that need the cloud.

Same books everywhere

Enter a sale on one computer and it appears on the others within seconds — nothing to copy, export or email around.

Staff logins you control

Add people by mobile number and tick exactly what each may do — sales, purchases, payments, items and more. See what they did in an activity list, and remove anyone at any time.

Keeps going if the internet drops

Lost your connection mid-invoice? Carry on working. What you entered is sent to the cloud as soon as you are back online.

GST billing and full accounting

Sales, purchases, returns, quotations, CGST/SGST/IGST invoices, double-entry ledgers, Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and the GST reports.

More than one company

Run several businesses from one sign-in. Each has its own books, its own people and its own settings.

Private to the people you add

Each company's books can be opened only by the people you have added to it. Your books are held in Google's Mumbai data centre.

Offline or Online?

Two versions. Pick the one that fits how you work.

They are separate products and can even be installed on the same computer. Neither one touches the other's data.
