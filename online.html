Getting started

From download to your first invoice in minutes.

01

Download and install

A single installer for Windows 10 and 11. There is no server to maintain and no database to configure.

02

Sign in once, then use your PIN

Verify your mobile number with a one-time code, or continue with your Google account, and choose a PIN. From then on Jurisbooks opens with your PIN alone. A new account begins with a free 15-day trial.

03

Create your company — or open an existing one

Set up your business in a minute, or sign in on another computer and open the same company: every entry is already there.

What you get

The full Jurisbooks engine, with the reach of the cloud.

One set of books, everywhere

A sale entered at the counter appears on every other computer within seconds. Nothing to copy, export or email.

Staff access, precisely controlled

Add people by mobile number or email and decide exactly what each may do — sales, purchases, payments, items and more. Review their work in the activity list and withdraw access at any time.

Uninterrupted, even offline

Jurisbooks opens and works without an internet connection, even at start-up. Everything you enter is sent to the cloud automatically when the connection returns.

GST billing and complete accounting

Sales, purchases, returns and quotations; CGST, SGST and IGST invoices; double-entry ledgers; Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and GST reports — on every plan.

Several businesses, one sign-in

Each company keeps its own books, people and settings, and you move between them in a click.

Always current, always backed up

Books are saved safely in the cloud and on your computer, with an optional extra copy in your own Google Drive. New versions of Jurisbooks install themselves.
