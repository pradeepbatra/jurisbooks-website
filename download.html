How to update

Four steps. Your data stays exactly as it is.

  1. Back up first. In the program: Business Settings → Backup & Restore.
  2. Close Jurisbooks completely.
  3. Run the new installer over the old one. Do not uninstall first, and do not choose to delete data. Use the installer for your own edition.
  4. Open Jurisbooks and check the new number under Business Settings → About & Updates.

Business Premium with office-network sharing: update every computer in the office to the same version.

Upgrading to a higher edition (for example Basic to Premium) works the same way: install the higher edition over the old one and enter your new licence key.
