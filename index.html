Why businesses choose Jurisbooks

Precision you can rely on. Simplicity you will enjoy.

01

Correct by design

Built on genuine double-entry accounting. A sale, a purchase, a receipt or a return updates stock, the party’s balance, cash or bank and your GST in a single step — so the Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet always agree.

02

Effortless every day

Raise a GST invoice in seconds. Tax, round-off and the customer’s outstanding balance are worked out for you, and the bill can go to your customer on WhatsApp in one click.

03

GST, handled properly

CGST and SGST or IGST applied automatically, HSN-wise and rate-wise summaries, B2B and B2C views, a clear net-payable figure when it is time to file, and ready-made files for GSTR-1 and e-way bills.

04

Nothing is lost

Work is saved as you go, backed up automatically and, with Online, kept in the cloud as well. A financial year can be closed and archived without disturbing the next.

05

People who answer

Talk to us on WhatsApp or by phone, in English or Hindi. We help you set up, bring your old data across, and stay with you as your business grows.
