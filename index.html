Why It's Different

Your data never leaves your computer.

Most billing software today runs through someone else's servers — your sales figures, customer details and margins sitting on a cloud you don't control. Jurisbooks was built the other way around.

100% local storage

Every invoice, ledger entry, and customer record is saved directly on your own computer — not uploaded, not synced, not stored anywhere else.

Zero network calls

The application is built to block outbound internet requests entirely. There is no server for your data to reach, even by accident.

No sign-up, no account

There's no login tied to a company database somewhere else. Your business data is yours, on your machine, under your control.

Backups on your terms

Automatic local backups run on their own schedule, saved to a folder you choose — never uploaded anywhere unless you decide to.
