About & Vision

Your books. Your choice of where they live.

Jurisbooks was built on a simple belief: a business's numbers belong to that business — not to whichever company happens to host the server. Some owners want their books on their own computer. Others want the same books on every computer they use. So Jurisbooks comes in two versions, with the same accounting underneath.

Jurisbooks Offline →Jurisbooks Online →
The Problem

Billing software usually means handing your numbers to someone else.

Most commercial billing and accounting tools are built cloud-first: your sales figures, customer lists, and margins live on a server you'll never see, governed by a company you have to trust indefinitely. That trade-off is treated as unavoidable, and you're rarely told there is a choice. We think where your books live should be your decision, made with clear facts.

The Approach

Two versions, one way of keeping books correctly.

Both versions run the same billing, double-entry accounting and GST reporting. The difference is where your books are kept — and you should know exactly which one you are choosing.

Jurisbooks Offline

On your own computer

A real application on one computer — not a website with an offline mode bolted on. There's no server it's designed to talk to, and outbound network requests are blocked at the application level, so nothing can leave by accident. No sign-up, and no internet needed.

See Jurisbooks Offline →
Jurisbooks Online

In the cloud, on every computer

The same books on every computer you use. Sign in with your mobile number, add your staff and choose what each person may do, and keep working even if the internet drops. Each company's books can be opened only by the people you add, and are held in Google's Mumbai data centre.

What Stays the Same

Four principles behind both versions.

01

Correctness over convenience shortcuts

Every document feeds a real double-entry ledger. Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, and Balance Sheet are checked to balance on every entry — not estimated, not assumed.

02

Privacy you can explain

With Offline there's no toggle for "don't send my data anywhere," because there's nowhere for it to go. With Online your books are held in one named place — Google's Mumbai data centre — and open only to the people you've added. Either way, you're told exactly where your data lives.

03

Built for how Indian businesses are actually taxed

GST — CGST, SGST, IGST, HSN codes, rate-wise and party-wise reporting — isn't an add-on module. It's part of the core billing flow from the first invoice.

04

Grows with the business, doesn't lock it in

Start with core billing. Add full accounting and GST reporting, and more companies, when the business is ready for them — and choose Online when more people or places need to work on the same books. Never a forced migration to "keep up."

Where We're Headed

Our vision

A business owner should be able to run their entire billing and accounting operation without ever wondering where their data actually lives or who can see it. Whether that's on their own computer or in the cloud, the answer should always be clear — and the choice theirs. Jurisbooks is our answer to that: accounting software built the way we'd want to use it ourselves — correct, honest about your data, and under the business's own control.
