About Jurisbooks

Books kept correctly should not be  hard work. sweat and blood

Jurisbooks was created on a simple conviction: every business, however small, deserves accounts that are exact, and software that makes keeping them feel effortless. We build GST billing and complete double-entry accounting for Indian businesses — precise underneath, and calm and clear on the surface.

Why we built it

Most businesses are asked to choose between simple and correct.

Billing tools are often quick to use but leave the accounts to be pieced together later; accounting packages are thorough but demanding. Jurisbooks removes that compromise. Each invoice you raise is posted to the right ledgers the moment you save it, so the stock, the party balances, the GST and the financial statements are complete and in agreement — without any extra effort from you.

Two editions

One accounting engine, the way you prefer to work.

Both editions share the same billing, inventory, double-entry accounting and GST reporting. They differ only in where your books are kept.

Jurisbooks Online

In the cloud, current on every computer

Sign in once with your mobile number or Google account, then open Jurisbooks with your PIN. Your books are saved safely in the cloud and stay identical on every computer; your staff work with the access you give them, and Jurisbooks keeps working when the internet does not.

Our principles

Four commitments behind every release.

01

Accuracy is not optional

Every document feeds a genuine double-entry ledger. The Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet are proven to balance on every entry — never estimated, never assumed.

02

Simplicity is a feature

The everyday work — a sale, a purchase, a receipt — should take seconds and need no accounting knowledge. The depth is there when you need it, and out of the way when you do not.

03

Made for Indian taxation

CGST, SGST and IGST, HSN codes, rate-wise, party-wise and B2B / B2C reporting are part of the core of Jurisbooks from the first invoice, not an add-on.

04

Growing with you

Begin with a single company and a single computer. Add companies, colleagues and capabilities as the business grows — without changing software or re-entering a single record.

Our vision

Exact books within reach of every Indian business.

We want every shopkeeper, trader and growing enterprise to know, at any moment, precisely where their business stands — what was sold, what is owed, what is in stock and what tax is due — without needing to be an accountant to find out. Jurisbooks is our commitment to that, one careful release at a time.
