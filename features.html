Features

One engine for billing, stock and accounts — built to be exact.

Jurisbooks is a single, integrated system. A sale you save updates stock, the customer’s account, cash or bank, the ledgers and your GST in one step — nothing is entered twice, and nothing drifts out of agreement.

Everything from Billing to Operations is included in both Jurisbooks and Jurisbooks Offline. The final two sections describe what each edition adds.

Jurisbooks (cloud) →Jurisbooks Offline →
Billing

Sales, purchases & GST invoicing

Every document a trading business needs, each connected to the same stock and party balances.

  • Sales, Purchases, Sales Returns, Purchase Returns, and Quotations (which never touch stock or balances until converted to a real sale)
  • Automatic CGST/SGST/IGST splitting based on buyer and seller state, with HSN/SAC codes per item
  • Five selectable invoice designs, plus a dedicated 80mm thermal receipt format, with live preview before you commit
  • Your own terms and a closing note on every invoice, with editable document titles (for example, “Tax Invoice” or “Cash Bill”)
  • Financial-year numbering (e.g. INV-26-27-0001) that you can adjust at any time
Accounting

Genuine double-entry books

A complete Chart of Accounts underlies every sale, purchase, receipt, payment and journal entry, so your figures are demonstrably consistent.

  • Chart of Accounts and Journal Vouchers for entries outside the standard documents
  • Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet, each with a live “Balanced” confirmation
  • CGST, SGST and IGST maintained in separate ledger accounts
  • Discounts and additional charges (freight, packing and the like) posted to their own accounts
GST

GST reporting you can file from

Every GST report is drawn directly from the same ledgers, so the figures always agree with your books.

  • Summary (3B): Output tax liability vs. input tax credit vs. net GST payable
  • Rate-wise and HSN-wise breakdowns of taxable value and tax collected
  • B2B / B2C invoice-wise listing, split automatically by whether the party has a GSTIN
  • Credit & Debit Notes from Sales/Purchase Returns, and Party-wise Tax summaries
Money & Ledgers

Every account, every party, always up to date

Balances update the moment a document is saved — no month-end catching up.

  • Named Bank Accounts and Cash, each with a real running balance and its own statement view
  • Transfers between your own accounts, kept separate from party transactions
  • Party Ledger with a summary footer (sales, purchases, received, paid, due) and FIFO-based Receivable/Payable Aging (0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ days)
  • Income and expense entries with categories of your own
Operations

Designed around the way businesses really work

The practical details that make a difference every day.

  • Several companies under one sign-in (Online) or one installation (Offline), each kept entirely separate
  • Stock movement by party, item-wise and party-wise cross-reports
  • Search on every list — receipts, payments, the cash & bank book, party statements
  • Financial-year shortcuts on every report, and year-end closing with a full archive of the year
Sharing & presentation

Documents your customers will notice

Professional output, and quicker ways to send it.

  • Send an invoice or a payment reminder on WhatsApp in one click
  • Item pictures on your catalogue and, if you wish, on the invoice
  • Barcode label printing and scanning at the counter (Premium plans)
  • Save any document or report as a PDF, or print original, duplicate and triplicate copies
Jurisbooks

The same books on every computer — and on your phone

Everything above, with the reach and resilience of the cloud.

  • Sign in once with your mobile number or Google account, then open Jurisbooks with a PIN
  • A sale entered on one computer appears on the others within seconds
  • Jurisbooks on your phone, free with every plan: make a bill, take a payment and share the invoice on WhatsApp from wherever you are — open app.jurisbooks.com on your phone today; the Android app is coming to Google Play soon
  • Staff logins with precise permissions, an activity list, and access you can withdraw at any time
  • Opens and works without internet; your work is sent automatically when the connection returns
  • Books saved safely in the cloud, with an optional additional copy in your own Google Drive
  • Automatic updates, and a free 15-day trial to begin
Jurisbooks Offline

Complete books on your own computer

Everything above, installed on one computer and independent of the internet.

  • Runs entirely on your computer; no connection is ever needed
  • A licence key issued for your business name and computer, with optional password protection
  • Automatic daily backups to a folder or drive you choose
  • Office network sharing with Business Premium — several computers working on one set of books
